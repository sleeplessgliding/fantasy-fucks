Regular season (weeks 1–13)

Kris was a 10–3, 1,848-point problem. Next-best record was 7–6. Next-best points was Seif at 1,625 — and Seif finished 6th. The league spent most of November arguing about four 7–5 teams and one 6–6 team chasing one last seat. Week 13 settled it: the three 7–5 playoff teams all lost, PJ won, and Neil was the odd man out on points for.

Won the draft: Kris, and it was not subtle. JSN at 3.02 was the gasp pick. Pickens at 6.09 finished as a WR2. JT at 2.09 was the regular-season points monster. Bijan at 1.02 did Bijan things, then dropped 39.9 in week 17 when it counted.

The waiver hits that actually mattered: Rico Dowdle (PJ — RB10, won him weeks), Drake Maye (Neil — the reason Neon Keon was a 7–6 team and not a 5–8 team), Tet was a draft pick but the rest of Neil’s roster was a waiver construction project.

Trades — the league only made two.

  • Week 4, Jake ↔ Andy: Jake got Ashton Jeanty and Chase Brown. Andy got David Montgomery. Andy had just taken Jeanty at 2.01.
  • Week 5, Jake ↔ Seif: Jake flipped Chase Brown to Seif for Zay Flowers. Seif got the 10th-round keeper who was RB-productive the rest of the way, including 29.1 in week 17. Jake kept Jeanty and made the playoffs. Andy kept Montgomery and November.

That is the entire trade deadline. Everyone else decided to lose on their own.

Team portraits

Kris Turner — Spitters not Quiters — Champion

10–3, 1,848 PF, pick 1.02, first title. Drafted Bijan, JT, JSN, Pickens, ETN and then mostly just… started them. Three losses, one of them the 197-point Seif game. Did not have to deal with the injury tax the rest of the league paid. The awards recap called him a believer in if you ain’t first, you’re last. The bracket agreed.

Joel Hernandez — Achane Reaction — 2nd

The WR room (Amon-Ra, Adams, later Stafford off waivers) got him to a 3-seed and a championship. Achane was a weekly problem. The final was a two-week mismatch. Stopping the three-peat is a real résumé line.

Jake Bowen — Egboogadaba — 3rd

#4 seed two years running. Javonte at 10.03 was the draft-night groan that became RB8. Henry finally showed up in week 17, which is a very Henry thing to do. Lost the semis in both weeks, not just the total. The 174-point week 5 was the regular-season peak.

PJ Hardcastle — Boswell that ends well. — 4th

Two-time defending champ, 2-seed, out in the semis. The roster was Cook, CeeDee, Rice, and a waiver WR1 he stole from Tony. Nabers lasted until he didn’t. The three-peat talk died in weeks 14–15. Fourth is a long way from a banner.

Neil Calvert — Neon Keon — 5th

7–6, missed on points. Rebuilt midseason with FAAB, rode Maye and McBride, drafted Tet in the 6th. The RB injuries never stopped. Week 13 vs Seif was the door closing. Tet looks like the keeper. The rest looks like a warning about spending $39 on Rachaad White.

Matt Seifert — Im sorry, Mc-Jackson, ooh — 6th

1,625 PF, 6–7. CMC was a monster. Bowers’s 43.3 week was the highest TE game of the year. Lamar was the letdown award. The November 4–1 run was a playoff team that started 2–6. Second in points is a cruel way to watch other people play in January.

CJ — Barbeque Gibbs — 7th

Gibbs went for 55.4. AJ Brown was a loyalty project. The team woke up in December, spoiled a couple of lock-in weeks, then ran up 541 consolation points. If the $100 pool paid on raw postseason scoring, he took it.

Andy — Mr. Turna 2-to-uh-Titanic — 8th

5–3 through eight weeks. Drafted Puka and Mahomes. Traded Jeanty and Chase Brown in week 4. Skattebo went supernova and then to IR. November: 1–4, 451 points, a 60-burger from Kris. The boat name was not ironic for long.

Tony Warner — It’s just a Ladd — 9th

Drafted Chase 1.01. Finished 5–8. Tyler Warren was the steal (8.10, TE4). The waiver habit was spend, drop, spend again — Pittman being the exhibit. Still beating Jake in week 13 was a nice souvenir. It did not change the portrait.

Pangy — Josh Allen’s New Phat Hog — Slutler

Allen was elite. Everyone else was on a trainer’s table. Saquon at 1.04, Woody Marks at $38, Michael Carter at $17, five guys unavailable by week 5, 4–9, last in points, last in the pool. The notes in week 13: “I don’t know man. Just do better?” That is the yearbook quote.
