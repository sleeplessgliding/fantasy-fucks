THE BEST OF THE BEST…

MOST VALUABLE PLAYER

Christian McCaffery - It’s been awhile since I just wrote in 30 for a player before the week started, but I was doing this all year for CMC. Amongst Flexes, CMC outscored the field by 28% - that’s unreal. Only Lamar came close in total points, in his own historic stretch, but with the premium at RB and deep field at QB, it’s CMC on the playoff-bound Return of the Mack. Honorable Mentions: Patriots D, Michael Thomas, Lamar Jackson Last Year: Conner, runners-up: Hill, McCaffrey, Kamara, Brees

BRIGHTEST FUTURE

Lamar Jackson - The odds-on favorite for (real life) MVP looks to be the Human Cheat Code 2.0, or 3.0 or something. He was a little lost in our league, with Kris outside the mix until late, but we’ll see that change in years to come. It’s tough to believe he’ll be available next year, as he’d be an 11th round pick keeper. Honorable Mentions: DJ Chark Jr, Nick Chubb, CMC*, Cook *CMC could win like every award but that’s not that interesting to read Last Year: Trubisky & Saquon, runner-up: Andrew Luck

BEST MOVE

Ian - A multi-step move started with Ian drafting WR depth, including Keenan Allen, then he picked up Chark which allowed him to flip Allen for Darren Waller, since Neil had an abundance of TE. Allen’s been erratic this year, and Waller makes Ian dangerous in all major lineup positions. The topper: the vision to replace Allen with Parker, who’s been ultra-steady. Honorary Mention: See Worst Move below… Last year: Saved by the Conner, Rick N’ More TDs

THE WORST OF THE WORST…

END OF THE ROAD

Todd Gurley II - It’s been real Todd, wish you the best. (Shades of Brandon Roy) All I know is the Rams would be foolish to keep up with the charade. The flashes in the pan were not bright, and they were far between. There were a couple runs where I talked myself into thinking there was something in the tank, but I’d rather be on this side of the take and be wrong (to be fair, I think I was in on this take by Week 2). Honorable Mention: Tom Brady, Larry Fitzgerald, David Johnson (probably) Last year: Tom Brady

MOST DISAPPOINTING

Juju Smith-Schuster - I really wanted to make this Baker and OBJ combined for the pleasure of it. But when I added Juju to Honorable Mentions, it was just too clear. No other player in the top tier failed to produce a boom game, and it’s not even been close with Juju. It’s been weeks since one would consider starting him, besides maybe against Miami. I’m not sure it’s all the QB either, the Steelers need another WR to give him some relief on double-teams. It’ll be very interesting to see where he ranks coming into next year. Honorable Mention: Baker & OBJ, David Johnson, Todd Gurley II Last season: Tight End depth / Gronk

WORST MOVE

Neil trading Nuk: PJ somehow swindled Nuk and Michel for Singletary and Fuller, who isn’t even on Neil’s team any longer (albeit after injury). Singletary has one week this year with 15+. While Michel’s not been much, Hopkins is WR4, with 15+ in every week since Week 4. Last year: drafting Bell (Andy), trading for Bell (Jake), believing in McCoy (Joel)

When playoffs start, a consolation bracket will start. The consolation bracket will have six teams, and two, two-week matchups.
