Draft Order

On Saturday, August 14th, we’ll live stream picking the draft order. This will give you plenty of time to make the wrong decision on who to keep, and if anyone’s ballsy enough, trade some picks!

Keeper Due Date

Saturday, August 21st, is the date you must submit your keepers to me by. I will update the league when I have them all (but this should help with draft strategy too, one week ahead of draft)!

Keeper Rules

Any player drafted in the 3rd round or later can be kept. The cost for the keeper is based on their round drafted, minus one. If you choose to repeat keep, you’ll pay in triplicate for your picks (not $ buy-in). If you want to keep a player that was undrafted, you can keep them at the cost of their projected round plus one round in the first ESPN Fantasy Mock Draft of the year (see below). Keeper Example: * First year: Keeper’s round-drafted, minus 1 * 1 round penalty * Second year: 1 x 3 = 3 round penalty * Third year: 3 x 3 = 9 round penalty * Fourth year: Cannot be re-kept as penalty would be -27 rounds Undrafted Example: * Chase Claypool is projected in the 8th round in the Mock Draft. * He can be kept as a 9th rounder, as if you drafted him in the 10th the previous year. * Next year, he would be a 7th rounder.

Keeper Rules

Any player drafted in the 3rd round or later can be kept. The cost for the keeper is based on their round drafted, minus one. If you choose to repeat keep, you’ll pay in triplicate for your picks (not $ buy-in). If you want to keep a player that was undrafted, you can keep them at the cost of their projected round plus one round in the first ESPN Fantasy Mock Draft of the year (see below). Keeper Example: * First year: Keeper’s round-drafted, minus 1 * 1 round penalty * Second year: 1 x 3 = 3 round penalty * Third year: 3 x 3 = 9 round penalty * Fourth year: Cannot be re-kept as penalty would be -27 rounds Undrafted Example: * Chase Claypool is projected in the 8th round in the Mock Draft. * He can be kept as a 9th rounder, as if you drafted him in the 10th the previous year. * Next year, he would be a 7th rounder.
