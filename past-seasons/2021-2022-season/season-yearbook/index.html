The wire, the wallet, the trades

Eleven trades, three of them in week 1. The league opened the season by rearranging itself.

The trades that actually mattered:

  • Week 1, Andy ↔ PJ: Andy got Thielen and Hunt. PJ got Zeke and Higbee. Opening-night swap.
  • Week 1, Jake ↔ Andy: Jake got Swift and Landry. Andy got Jamaal Williams and Robbie Chosen. Swift on the finals team.
  • Week 1, PJ ↔ Neil: PJ got Tyreek and Tannehill. Neil got Murray and Gus Edwards. Tyreek 47.6 in week 4 already wore the new laundry. The awards-season MVP on the 1-seed’s roster.
  • Week 7, Neil ↔ Ian: Neil got Diggs and Pitts. Ian got Waller and Sutton. The 6-seed shopping for a spark.
  • Week 7, Kris ↔ Jake: Kris got Conner, Antonio Brown, Claypool. Jake got Chubb, Gaskin, Julio. Jake’s RB room after Henry.
  • Week 8, CJ ↔ PJ: CJ got Higbee and Damien Harris. PJ got Gronk. The 1-seed adding a name.

Joel sent Burrow to Ian for Edmonds in week 7 — Burrow scored 9 draft-class points for a reason. Jake also grabbed Michael Thomas and later Pittman went to Andy for Toney. The midseason notes were thinner than later years; the transaction log is the recap.

Team portraits

Tony Warner — Kupp half empty — Champion

8–5, 1,707 PF, last pick of round 1, first on the draft board, first in January. Kupp 304, Ekeler, Fournette 44 in week 12, 323 in the semis, 266 in the final. The joke name on a title team.

Jake Bowen — Ms. New Jeudy — 2nd

Worst draft class, 4-seed, beat the 1-seed, lost the final by 23. Allen, Kelce, Henry, Swift via Andy. Week 13 was 88 points. The two weeks after that were a finals ticket.

PJ Hardcastle — DK Metca$h — 3rd

1,799 PF, JT 53.4, Tyreek from Neil in week 1. The 1-seed that lost both semis weeks. Took 3rd. The points were a year early for the three-peat.

Matt Seifert — Kittles ‘n Bits — 4th

Cook at 1.02, Brady, Deebo, Chase. 8–5. Week 15 of the semis was 96. The third title was one year later.

Joel Hernandez — Take Mahomes Tonight — 5th

CMC at 1.01 for 128 points, Mahomes, a 172 in week 13, 7–6. One win from a five-team 8–5. Burrow scored 9 and then got traded to Ian.

Neil Calvert — Unpaid Bills — 6th

Opened by mailing Tyreek to PJ. Added Diggs in week 7. 6–7 with 1,588 PF. Playoff scoring, not a playoff record. Then 534 consolation points.

Chris Johnson — Russell'n up a Cook out — 7th

Jefferson at 1.04, Kamara, 1,717 PA. 6–7. The consolation 176 and 164 were the best two weeks of his year, in January.

Andy — Mixon it up wit ya Mama — 8th

Herbert, Mixon, Zeke, CeeDee, a top-four draft class, 5–8. The midseason notes had him 10th. He climbed out of the cake and onto the 8th-place step.

Kris Turner — HOLLYWOOD or BROWN? — Slutler

Chubb at 1.06, Lamar, 5–8, 393 consolation points. Last of ten. The first of three straight cakes, if you count the next two years.

Ian — I Don’t Care — 10th

fcnchuck in Sleeper, Ian in the week-13 notes. Stafford, Diggs, 4–9. Last archived season in this chair. Pangy took it the next year.
