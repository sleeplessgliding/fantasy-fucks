Week 11 Power Rankings

Updated 2022-08-05

🏈 Week 11! 🏈

🧙🏻‍♂️ Waiver Wire Wizard: Neil swings for the fences with $40 on AJ “The Sauce” Dillon 🧙🏻‍♂️

🤼‍♂️ Matchup of the Week: Seif (6-4) v. Joel (5-5) 🤼‍♂️

HUGE game in the middle. Joel seemingly needs this one to stay in the mix. Seif can create that space.

What to watch for? Miles Sanders and CMC in Joel’s backfield - his starters are finally together again. AJ Brown is so intriguing right now too.

💪 Week 11 Power Rankings 🍺

1. PJ (7-3) - Another high scorer week, let’s try not to feed his ego too much. But he got it done without a high scorer at any position, shows the average strength for all positions.

2. Seif (6-4) - Another solid week from Seif. As Gibson, Kittle and Samuel all got going again, and Cook back in the lineup, this team’s looking reeel good.

3. Jake (6-4) - Huge win against Tony. Chubb picks up the slack but I’m thinnn at WR now with Hopkins off the reservation.

4. Tony (6-4) - Still in second place but the close loss hurts. Couple question marks remain but he could be getting stronger at the right time with Barkley back in the lineup.

5. Neil (5-5) - The big spender! I like the sense of urgency to grab Dillon with no other RB to start on the roster. and it could really pay off if he’s out more than a week. Good time to get Robinson back too!

6. Kris (5-5) - Hanging around! It seems like this was a week KJT needed a win, and he’ll be without Lamar, oh no! When you look at this lineup you think how could he win with that? But it might be enough against shorthanded Neo.

7. Joel (5-5) - Big week falls short, against arch-rival PJ too! Sliding but still has a shot at the big one.

8. Chris (4-6) - Must wins from here on out for technically-still-alive CJ. Bad time to not have Kamara, but great time to have snagged Ingram, well done.

9. Andy (3-7) - He’s got zero bucks!

10. Ian (3-7) - He’s got zero good players!
