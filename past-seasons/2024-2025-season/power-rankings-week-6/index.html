Power Rankings (Week 6)

Updated 2024-10-20

Matchup of the Week: Achane’s Cocaine Train (1-4) vs. Josh Allen’s Fat Hog (2-3) Typically you gotta be 8-4 to have a chance at the playoffs. This is a big one.

CJ’s been acting with desperation, Neil’s been trusting his team. What will happen!?

What to watch: * For Neil, AJ Brown returns after Neil’s lost each week he’s been out… right in time for Nico to go out. * For Chris, he’s starting Tank Dell in Nico’s absence!

Waiver Wire Wizards and Weiners: * Weiner: Jake - Tank Bigsby $13 - I hate the Jaguars, they’re so poorly coached and Lawrence is a dummy - and I have two of their backs now. * Weiner: Joel - Kyler Murray $12 - It’s not a bad pickup, it’s just that no one else bid on him. * Wizard: PJ - Buccaneers D - $1 - They’re playing against Spencer Rattler in his first start, yeesh! Loved this snipe. I immediately picked them up in my other league when I saw PJ’s move.

1. Seif (4-1, PF: 704.06, $97 FAAB) ⬆️⬆️

Nabers, who? Seif rips off another high-scoring week. Impressive to have spent less than a dollar a week.

2. Pangy (4-1, PF: 579.20, $2 FAAB) ⬆️⬆️⬆️

Winning matters and Pangy’s got four in a row. $2 FAAB - yikes!

3. Jake (4-1, PF: 582.70, $35 FAAB) ⬆️⬆️

Two highest-scoring weeks, two under 100, somehow 4-1. Off to a nice start with Purdy and Deebo.

4. PJ (3-2, PF: 624.02, $29 FAAB) ⬇️⬇️⬇️

Very close battle in the rankings for 4/5, but PJ has top-two depth. Did you see Henry get stopped for a safety? That’s a bad omen for any fantasy team.

5. Andy (3-2, PF: 635.96, $18 FAAB) ⬇️⬇️⬇️

No now tries harder than Andy. What do we think about Harrison after five weeks? IDK.

6. Kris (2-3, PF 546.12, $68 FAAB) ⬆️

Kris is skyrocketing! What a trade, stealing Kittle and the best WR of all time, Darnell Mooney, even with Kelce looking pretty solid this week.

7. Joel (2-3, PF 552.30, $44 FAAB) ⬆️⬆️

Starting three Jags, I can’t stop thinking about that, since the JAGs have won one game in their last 10.

8. Neil (2-3, PF 586.80, $78 FAAB) ⬇️⬇️

Loser of three straight and the best player going to the IR (Nico), while Josh Allen finally looks human.

9. CJ (1-3, PF 422.06, $61 FAAB) ⬇️

Daniels is god, but the WR room on this team is uh not so good.

10. Tony (0-5, 493.26, $63 FAAB) ↔️

Tucker’s back!

Good luck to everyone besides PJ!
