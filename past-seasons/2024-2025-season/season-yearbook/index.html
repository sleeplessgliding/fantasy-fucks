The waiver hits that actually mattered: Baker at $1 (PJ), Chase Brown (Andy — drafted 11.01, dropped after the slow start, then the RB1 in Cincinnati with 17.8 per game from week 4 on), Chuba Hubbard (Andy, $13), Bucky Irving (the other surprise RB). Seif’s Guerendo swing was the last-week headline.

Trades — seven of them, the messy ones:

  • Week 5, Andy ↔ CJ: Andy got Amon-Ra and McBride. CJ got CMC, Jacobs, and Kelce, plus $10 FAAB. Andy paid real capital to move off the 1.01 corpse.
  • Week 5, Andy ↔ Kris: Andy got Kelce and Aaron Jones. Kris got Kittle and Mooney. Kris’s “nice add via trade” in the obituaries is this one.
  • Week 7, Jake ↔ CJ: Jake got CMC (the remains) and Otton. CJ got Montgomery and Addison. The CMC tour continued.
  • Week 8, Andy ↔ PJ: Andy sent Mike Evans for Amari Cooper and Diontae Johnson. Cooper disappeared. Diontae got cut. The championship preview called it immediately. PJ’s Mayfield–Evans stack went to the final.
  • Week 8, CJ ↔ PJ: PJ got Achane. CJ got DeVonta. The champ added another toy.

Andy built a finals team by trading the brand names and streaming the right RBs. PJ built a three-peat by collecting Evans and Achane and starting Baker for a dollar.

Team portraits

PJ Hardcastle — 8===D — Champion

9–4, 1,733 PF, third straight title. Drafted Chase and Henry, bought Baker for a dollar, paid $56 for Chubb’s ghost, then collected Evans and Achane in week 8. Lost week 14 of the semis and won the series anyway. The championship preview said the lineup looked sus. The lineup went 287.

Andy — Mr. Turna 2-to-uh-4 — 2nd

First finals. Drafted CMC 1.01 and still got here, which is a magic trick. Lamar at 4.10 was the MVP. The waiver RBs (Chase Brown, Chuba) were the engine. The Evans trade was the scar. Week 15’s 186.7 was the loudest game of the postseason. The last two weeks were quieter.

Jake Bowen — Jahmyr We Go Again — 3rd

#4 seed by 2.18 points after losing to 3–10 on Thanksgiving. Gibbs made the team name. ETN at 2.01 made the letdown ballot. Won week 14 of the semis, lost the two-week, took 3rd when Tee Higgins went for 40 in week 17.

Matt Seifert — Wont you be my Nabers — 4th

Won the draft. 8–5, 1,707 PF, a week-2 170 and a week-11 176. Saved FAAB for Guerendo in week 13. Mixon on bye in the semis. The 3-seed that looked like a heavyweight in October and a depth chart in January.

Chris Johnson — Bubble Boy — 5th

2–6, then 5–0. Beat PJ by 51, beat Andy in week 13, stacked six wins, and lost a playoff seat by a catch on Monday night. The obituaries blamed the week he didn’t start a defense. The math blamed 2.18 points.

Pangy — Sweet Brotha Numpsay — 6th

Saquon was a weekly event (33, 34, 33, 46). Kyren and Stroud faded. 7–6 and a 150 in week 13 was not enough PF. Terry was the keeper note. The team that drafted like a playoff roster and finished next to one.

Neil Calvert — Josh Allen’s Fat Hog — 7th

Allen was a monster (51.9 in week 14). Godwin and Nico were not available. 6–7 with 1,563 PF — playoff scoring, not a playoff record. Lasted to the final two of the $50 survivor with Andy. The later rules page still has him winning the $100 consolation pool.

Kris Turner — Swift Love Kelce — Slutler

In the mix until week 13. Reed in the 8th was the snipe. Kittle via trade was the adult move. 6–7, then 450 consolation points, last in the pool. The 2023–24 Slutler, renewed. The notes asked if he could build on this to make the playoffs next year. He did. He also won the whole thing the year after that.

Joel Hernandez — Griddy Griddy Bang Bang — 9th

3–10, 1,764 PA. The roster on paper (Jefferson, Cook, BTJ, Pacheco when healthy) was not a 3–10 roster. The matchup luck was. Then he scored 594 in the consolation weeks, which is a very Joel way to finish 9th.

Tony Warner — LaPorta Potty — 10th

0–8. Bijan at 1.02. Tucker, the kicker you used to treat like a bye week. Two wins in the last three, including the Jake spoiler, which is the kind of ending that makes a 3–10 look like a choice. Voted most likely to fall asleep at the draft. The notes said be there next year, big dogg.
