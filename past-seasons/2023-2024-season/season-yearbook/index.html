The wire, the wallet, the trades

Twelve trades. Jake’s awards write-up called his own team Trade-O-Rama and printed an intervention number. Andy was first to run out of FAAB, week 8.

The trades that actually mattered:

  • Week 1, Tony ↔ Joel: Tony got Kelce and Miles Sanders. Joel got Jacobs and Goedert. The awards page said Joel won it on the numbers. Tony still had Allen and CMC.
  • Weeks 2–4, Jake’s run: Jeudy from Kris for Cooks and a pile of FAAB. Then Mahomes for Mostert and Love. Then Kamara to CJ for Lockett. Then Achane to Andy for Ekeler. The original roster was gone by Halloween.
  • Week 6, Pangy ↔ Andy: Pangy got Deebo, Achane, Stroud, and the 49ers. Andy got Swift. The November 5–0 had a receipt.
  • Week 9, Andy ↔ PJ: PJ got Amon-Ra. Andy got Andrews, Pierce, and Flowers. The notes said the Mandrews side did not work. PJ’s side went to the final.
  • Week 10, CJ ↔ Seif: CJ got Henry. Seif got Bijan. The 1-seed added a closer; the 5-seed added a name.
  • Week 11, Neil ↔ PJ: Neil got Aiyuk. PJ got Rachaad White. Last trade of the year, last-place dart.

Jake also flipped Lockett and McBride back to Kris in week 10 for Thielen and Waller. CJ’s Kamara add (week 4) was the awards honorable mention for when it mattered.

Team portraits

PJ Hardcastle — 8=========D — Champion

8–5, 1,538 PF, the fewest points of any playoff team, then 318 in the final. Drafted Jefferson and Fields and watched both die. Collected Amon-Ra in week 9. Lost to Kris by 44 in week 13. The awards preview said luck. The box score said 188.

Pangy — Sweet Brotha Numpsay — 2nd

3–5, then 5–0. The Achane / Deebo / Stroud haul in week 6 was the second-half roster. Beat Tony twice when it mattered. Week 16 was 86 points in a championship. The bounce-back year that bounced once more.

Tony — JA MONTG — 3rd

Won the draft. CMC, Allen, a six-game streak, 1,755 PF, the 2-seed. Lost week 13 to Pangy and both semis weeks to Pangy. Took 3rd over the 1-seed because someone had to. Most valuable manager on the awards page. Not the champion.

Chris Johnson — Not Diggin that mustard — 4th

9–4, 1,764, Keenan, Diggs, Henry via trade, Dak down the stretch. Regular-season first place. Week 15: 79 points. The cruelest 1-seed in the archive.

Matt Seifert — Tua infinity and Bijan — 5th

First self-draft. Chase 52 in a 209-point week. Lowest PA in the league. 7–6. Three first-round names on the roster at some point and still dancing in the 5th-place pool. The notes were happy he drafted it himself. Happier he missed.

Andy — Mr. TURNA 2-2-uh-4 — 6th

6–7, 9th in PF, first out of FAAB. The Achane add from Jake, the Andrews add from PJ. Amon-Ra left. +40 almost saved him from the cake. Seven points.

Joel Hernandez — Hurts Donut — 7th

5–8 with 1,567 PF. The Kelce trade for Jacobs, a 180 in week 4, Puka in the room. The notes gave him a 90-point Slutler buffer. He used it.

Jake Bowen — Exotic Engrams — 8th

Opened 170. Traded five times. Fields was the letdown. Tyreek was the only thing the draft got right. The awards page said call 1-800-877-8902.

Kris Turner — Love Heem — Slutler

Last in PF, first in PA, 9th on wins, last in the pool. Chubb 23. The Mostert / Love haul from Jake was the good trade. Seven points from Andy was the math. He wore it again the next year, then won the whole thing the year after that.

Neil Calvert — Unpaid Bills — 10th

4–9. CeeDee 265, Lamar, a late Aiyuk dart, and a side bet with Seif on points from week 7 on. Last in the standings, 2nd in the Slutler pool. The notes said don’t sleep on the last seed. The last seed scored 526 after the season ended.
