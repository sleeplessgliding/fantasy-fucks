The wire, the wallet, the trades

Nine trades. One of them was a roster swap dressed as a deal.

The trades that actually mattered:

  • Week 3, Andy ↔ Neil: Andy got Kelce, Patterson, Cooks. Neil got Jefferson, Knox, Koo. The 1.04 left the building. Neil’s board got Jefferson and still finished 9th.
  • Week 4, PJ ↔ Kris: PJ got Henry, Kittle, Jacobs, CeeDee, and Rodgers. Kris got Cook, Mahomes, Mattison, and Pitts. The monster. PJ was the 2-seed after it. Kris was 3–10.
  • Week 6, Kris ↔ PJ: Kris got Hopkins and Rodgers. PJ took Mahomes back. The return label.
  • Week 6, PJ ↔ Joel: PJ got Tyreek. Joel got Jacobs. Both made the playoffs. Joel made the final.
  • Week 7, Jake ↔ Neil: Jake got Jefferson, Lamar, Njoku, Gordon. Neil got Saquon, Dak, Gabe Davis, Pitts. The other Jefferson tour.
  • Week 9, Andy ↔ Tony: Andy got JuJu. Tony got Aaron Jones. Tony’s last meaningful add before 43 points.

Jake also flipped Hockenson to Kris in week 4 and later grabbed Waller from CJ. The waiver story the notes remember is PJ spending real FAAB on Kenneth Walker. The box scores remember the Kris trade.

Team portraits

Matt Seifert — The real BSD — Champion

7–6, 1,761 PF, the 4-seed that was the 1-seed in every category except wins. Allen, Adams, Ekeler. Beat Pangy, beat Joel, won both championship weeks. Third title. The preview hoped to end the third ring. It did not.

Joel Hernandez — The Hurts Locker — 2nd

CMC at 1.01, Hurts, Tyreek, a November 4–1, a semis win over PJ. Then the injured-QB championship and the shotgun. Lost 220–189. The 3-seed that got all the way there.

PJ Hardcastle — 8=========D — 3rd

9–4. Emptied Kris’s roster in week 4, took Mahomes back in week 6, added Tyreek the same week. Lost the semis by 14. Scored 535 after the season “ended.” The title wait was one more year.

Pangy — Sweet Brotha Numpsay — 4th

10–3. Burrow, Mixon, Chubb, Amon-Ra. Mixon 55 in week 9. Out in the first round to the points leader. The best regular season of the year and the quietest January.

Andy — Jus-Jonzin-4-Herb — 5th

7–6 with 1,463 PF. Drafted Jefferson, traded Jefferson, added Kelce, lost week 13 to Neil by 3. Same record as the champion. Not the same sport.

Chris Johnson — Wheelin & Thielen — 6th

JT at 1.02. 6–7. Waller for James Robinson. The 1.02 that became a mid-table finish.

Tony — Can you Diggs it? — Slutler

Diggs was real. The rest of the week-14 lineup was not. 43.36. The notes closed the book early.

Jake Bowen — One Snake No Kupp — 8th

The team name was the season. Kupp died, Jefferson arrived in week 7, Lamar too. 5–8. A 153 to open the year and a lot of 100s after that.

Neil Calvert — Unpaid Bills — 9th

Kamara at 1.10, a 184, Jefferson via Andy, Saquon via Jake. 4–9, 1,397 PF. Then 517 consolation points, first in the pool he did not need to win.

Kris Turner — BROWNS Fantasy — 10th

Henry at 1.03, a 174 in week 4, then the trade that built PJ’s 2-seed. 3–10. The draft class ranked 4th. The standings did not care.
