Power Rankings (Week 3)

Updated 2022-09-29

Matchup of the Week: Show Me the Mooney (1-1) v. 8D (0-2)

The classic Seif/PJ rivalry has a little more on the line this week, as starting 0-3 makes the road to the playoffs TOUGH.

Waiver Wire Wizard: Gerald Everett $9. Seif lost the MOTW last week by a somewhat thin margin, and got zero from TE. Sees his opportunity and strikes. (I thought my $1 may be enough lol)

Week Three Power Rankings

1. Pangy (2-0) ⬆️

It’s hard not to talk about St. Brown, he’s been incredible through two, but the eight-game stretch is extra incredible. Add Chubb to the mix, and YP gets the big win over Tony’s pretty solid week.

2. Tony (1-1) ⬆️

Tough loss, but Tony can chase it with Diggs touchdowns, holy moly. Hope to see Pittman back in the lineup last week as the gamble with Campbell cost Tony big.

3. Andy (2-0) ⬆️

Mike Williams slid in the draft and now in hindsight, I can’t see why? Allen was a walking hamstring injury ready to happen. Pair it with Herbie, and hope he starts feeling better.

4. Joel (1-1) ⬆️

I really can’t believe how open Tyreek was last week, but I guess that is him in a nutshell. Joel may have something special with his Miami and Philly duos.

5. Matt (1-1) ⬇️

Waddle’s 40 bomb and JA17’s four-TD beauty go squandered. But Chase has a human week and Kmet gooses, Matt looses. Still a solid week with many sub-par performances to keep Seif in the top five.

6. Neil (1-1) ⬇️

DJ Moore didn’t get launched directly into the sun! Honestly I feel good for him. Neil falls with the win, but it’s more about what we see above him. The cartilage injury Kamara is dealing with is very troubling for a RB, and Rip needs him to start cooking.

7. Jake (1-1) ⬇️

RIP Trey Lance. Feeling okay about Stafford in there now, but clearly QB and TE are weak spots for me. I drafted that way but it doesn’t make it feel any better. Too many chips on Hock, might be time to start working the wire for a replacement.

8. CJ (1-1) ⬆️

Curtis Samuel is really Deebo’ing it like his nickname in Sleeper, loving that waiver pickup. This team is all over the place right now, but it didn’t keep CJ to be the larger J versus PJ. Also, is Zeke done? Hide and Zeke, where are you?

9. 8D (0-2) ⬇️

The bright spot has been Thomas’ resurgence. The not-so-bright spot is RB2, dunno what the plan is there.

💩 Hollywood Browns (0-2)

Smart money is always on DJ Chark. - No one

Good luck this week everyone besides Andy. Stay hungry! - 🐍
