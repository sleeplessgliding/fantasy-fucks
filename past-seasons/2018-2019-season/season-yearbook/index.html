Season Yearbook

Updated 2026-08-22

Regular season

The final power rankings on the season page are the standings. Neil clinched first and sat. The last playoff chair was Joel vs PJ: Joel wins and he’s in; Joel loses and PJ is in with a win. Both drew bottom-feeders. Joel had just traded Cam for McCoy. PJ was first in points and allergic to wins. PJ got in. Seif needed a miracle and did not get one.

Who won the draft

No pick list. The awards are blunt anyway.

Won the waiver, which is the draft: James Conner, $3, the day after the draft, Neil. MVP “by a longshot.” Hill, McCaffrey, Kamara, and Brees all had cases. Conner is the value sentence. The other nine managers are in the “how dumb” clause.

Lost the draft: Andy. Bell at 1.01, McCoy in the 3rd, Landry in the 4th, Olsen in the 5th. “Doomed before the season started.” Jake’s own scar is trading Ertz “for literally nothing (and Jones but to a lesser degree).” Joel’s is believing in McCoy.

Brightest future: Trubisky and Saquon, both from Seif’s squad, Trubisky already on his second tour of the wire. Andrew Luck honorable mention. The “end of the road” aside was Brady no longer a QB1 and Rodgers going on-and-off — with a note that next year you might draft Jackson and Mayfield over both.

Who lost the season in November

Joel, in public, with a receipt. Two weeks left, needing both wins, he traded Cam Newton to Neil for LeSean McCoy. McCoy took a bye, then the Jags, then Joel’s bench. Joel started Jameis the week it mattered. The notes asked Seattle friends to check on PJ if he missed. PJ did not miss. Joel did.

Andy had already lost the season in August. The November version was “too little, too late” on a 3–7 with nice waiver activity. Seif never got a two-game streak. Kris was “technically still alive” and not really.

The wire, the wallet, the trades

Best moves: Neil, and not only Conner. He acquired McCoy and flipped him for Cam. Kittle and Dalvin Cook for an IR’d Jordan Howard and a Tate who had already been cashed. The Cam-for-McCoy deal is the one “the league is still investigating.” It made Neil’s QB room and ended Joel’s season.

Andy’s best moves are in the same awards section as his worst ones. He flipped Bell into Ertz and Jones (who then went to Jake), flipped McCoy for Breida. Those three won him a game. He finished with three.

Waiver crumbs the ranks bothered to name: Dak $12, Dede Westbrook $1, Marquez Valdes-Scantling $7, Dez $1 (PJ), Jets D $6, an RB for Ian at $19 when he needed one.

Worst moves, again: Andy’s draft. Jake trading Ertz. Joel believing in McCoy. The 2019 awards page still listed all three as last year’s graveyard.

Slutler

The notes do not run a Slutler preview in 2018 the way they do later. They run a 10th-place obituary:

Andy, Rick n more tds, the Curse of Le’Veon Bell. Brooklyn Dodgers, wait ’til next year. Three wins. Active, chatty, doomed in August. A week-11 table had him 8th and CJ 10th; the final ranks put Andy last. If someone else wore it, the archive does not say. This page will not invent a different name.

Team portraits

Jake Bowen — House on Tyreek Hill — Champion

First title. Hill in the team name, McCaffrey on the roster, Ebron surprising the league, a traded-away Ertz in the awards shame column. He wrote the season. He also won it.

Neil Calvert — Saved by the Conner — 11–1

The regular-season champion. $3 Conner, Cam via Joel, Kittle, Cook. Champagne parade, last week off, no ring. The first great “won the year until January” team in the notes.

Ian — Darnold Duck — semis

7–4, a 145-point streak, Fournette alive again, $19 on a dart RB. The other half of Jake’s preview. Still in the league through 2021.

PJ Hardcastle — Touchdown My Pants — semis

First in points, last into the bracket. The notes told Seattle to check on him. He was fine. The title took two more years.

Joel Hernandez — Take Mahomes Tonight — 5th

Mahomes was a football god and a spectator. The McCoy trade is the 2018 cautionary tale, cited for years. Fortnite skins mentioned. Jameis started.

Matt Seifert — Saquona Matata — 6th

Defending champ, talking shit, Saquon on the roster, never a two-game run. The notes promised he would be back. He won it the next year.

Tony — Hunt for a Playoff Miracle

Hopkins room, no identity, 7th. The team Jake did not want to play and then did not have to.

Chris Johnson — The Johnson Fitz (II)

Old Fogies. Last place for a week, 8th at the end. The notes guessed he would go younger in 2019.

Kris — Luck You

Luck, Gronk, Gurley. Least active, still a spoiler, 9th. The gal-with-Gurley-and-Gronk joke is in the awards.

Andy — Rick n more tds — last

Bell, McCoy, Landry, Olsen. Then the good trades that won him one of three games. The notes said if he keeps showing up he could be a dark horse. He kept showing up.
