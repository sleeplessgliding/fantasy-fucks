Power Rankings & Midseason Awards (Week 8)

Updated 2026-10-31

Tony and Kris had a battle worthy of first place, coming down to MNF, with Ceedee propelling Tony into first place, handing Kris his first loss in almost a year. Thank you, Tony.

At the bottom Pangy murdered Joel, who’s self-made LLM is going to have to find him an outfit for Slutler next year.

Reminder: Week Eleven is the last week to make trades. Get your deals done.

Midseason Awards

MVP

Jaxon Smith-Njigba Kris Back-to-back

Is this cd skipping? Well, it feels like it, with JSN coming in at #1 overall and keeping Kris’ team on auto-pilot.

Honorable Mention Jahmyr Gibbs (Kris) — Also on Kris, and #2 overall. Unanimous (mostly) #1 picks don’t usually do what Gibbs is doing. It’s clear that’ll deliver fantasy championships, even if the projected 500+ touches bury his career too early.

Repeat winner — 2025: Jaxon Smith-Njigba (Kris)

LVP

Saquon Barkley Jake Back-to-back

While their are some guys in the top 15 picks that have been up and down, no one’s been as bad as Barkley compared to draft capital. I know he’s on my team, but I don’t think this is a narrow lens thing. Even last week, he flashed in his brief return, only to exit with another injury, but he’ll suit up again this week to make sure he can torpedo more weeks.

Honorable Mention Omarion Hampton (Neil) — Neil locked his keeper in at 3.3, to a seemingly great value. He only has one 15+ week (against the terrible LV D), hasn’t eclipsed 63% snap share, and doesn’t come close to passing the eye test, even on this shit team.

Repeat winner — 2025: Saquon Barkley (Pangy)

Breakout Stud

Matthew Golden Tony

2nd year, 1st round pick is really looking like it, even while leaving bread on the table with drops. He’s in an offense that isn’t afraid to air it out and he’s showing why the pack invested their first round-one WR pick in however many years it was. Passes eye test for sure.

Honorable Mention Isaiah Likely (Tony) — We’d seen it for years, but like real estate, it’s location location location in this case. He’s been QB proof, even, producing at such a high clip with Dart, Winston, and now McCarthy (all first rounders - shrug).

2025 (Breakout Stars): Emeka Egbuka & Cam Skattebo

Slept On

D’Andre Swift Tony

Booted from Detroit with distain. Pretty fucking soliid in his one year in PHI. And now he’s a top-ten fantasy back in CHI, amongst the Bears’ #1 rushing attack. Super high floor.

Honorable Mention Jaylen Warren (Pangy) — Benefit of an injury to Dowdle, but he’s shown some fucking juice this year, in a middle-ish offense. Can that continue with Dowdle back in the mix - definitely possible.

Last given 2018: Patrick Mahomes

Most Surprising (in a good way)

George Kittle Seif

(and most guys who had season-ending injuries last year) I mean, it was like yesterday when Kittle blew out his achilles, and he doesn’t look any different than last year. It’s extremely impressive, considering the attention he gets with the Niners’ entire receiving corps in the hospital.

Honorable Mention Christian Watson (Seif) — Definitely a preseason hype target, but he’s been incredible and healthly… I mean, how could I not say most surprising for this guy when he just played a game where he broke a rib, went to the hospital in an ambulance and FINISHED THE GAME. Insane.

2024: Allen Lazard

Most Surprising (in a bad way)

Colston Loveland Pangy

The preseason darling on analysts boards has been extreeeemely mid, though the last two week’s production could be the sign of changing tides (before last week, his standard scoring high was 5.7).

Honorable Mention Justin Jefferson (Andy)

2024: Marvin Harrison Jr.

Goodbye, My Lover

A.J. Brown Seif Back-to-back

Last year’s winner is back and looks completely washed, even after coming back from his once or twice- yearly hamstring pull. Maye has indeed not been his savior, and maybe Hurts deserves less blame.

Repeat winner — 2025: DJ Moore, AJ Brown & Tyreek Hill
