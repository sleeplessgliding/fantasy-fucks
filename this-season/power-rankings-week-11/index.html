“I wish I knew how to quit you.” – - Brokeback Mountain dude. That's the theme of this week - what guys can we not quit, for better or worse.

All of us have something we can’t let go of. A player we drafted, a one aquired in a trade, someone who is surely going to pop the second we drop them, or, just a fan favorite.

Claude and I went through all six seasons of this league on Sleeper (2021 to now) to pull some data points, but I also know there's more in the lost ESPN seasons...

Also... there are some big changes this week in the rankings. Four teams moved three spots, so the arrows got bigger this week to match. Three weeks are left and only four teams make the playoffs. Joel and I are officially out (see the Obituaries at the bottom).

The trade deadline is this week, and nobody has made a trade yet. Can’t quit your guys, huh?

Pangy’s got the hottest team in the league, and Kris just bounced back with a great week. Something’s got to give here, and it comes at the worst time for Kris, who will be without JSN.

What to watch

  • QBs – Mahomes, off his best week of the year (and best in a long time), versus Joe Cool, the current odds-on favorite for MVP, coming off the hottest stretch of his career.

  • Number ones – Gibbs vs. Chase. Best of the best, and either could break it open.
  • Last one – Kris doesn’t look to have a solid plan for WR2 right now… could be a wild card or a joker.
