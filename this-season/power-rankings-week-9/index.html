Matchup of the Week

Andy

vs
Pangy
Josh Allen’s New Phat Hog

3-5

Pangy’s back for a second straight week, and he earned it. After a 1–5 start that included the lowest score of the season (58.3), he’s dropped 167 and 207 back to back, and the 207 is the highest score anyone has put up all season. Then he went shopping: $34 on Tyler Allgeier Wednesday, the biggest waiver bid of the season, outbidding Joel ($11) and Kris ($6). Andy is the opposite: never below 100, never above 138, and he’s lost two games by a combined 1.6 points. He could easily be 5–3. Instead both of these guys sit at 3–5, a game behind PJ and Neil at 4–4 in the race for the last playoff spot, and only one of them stays in the conversation. The loser is 3–6 with four games left, which is fantasy for “start thinking about keepers.”

What to watch

  • Pangy bought his way out of the bye. Pollard and Warren, his two starting RBs and 50 points last week, are both on bye, and Jeremiyah Love is on IR. So he paid $34 for Allgeier, who’s first on Arizona’s depth chart with Love out, and dropped Malik Washington to make room. Pangy bought his own injured rookie’s replacement. It’s Allgeier at Seattle and Woody Marks at the Chargers, with $28 of FAAB left for the rest of the season.
  • The Bengals get the Falcons. Burrow, Chase and Higgins put up 108 last week. On the other side is Andy’s Drake London (1.8 last week). Andy needs Atlanta to win a shootout, or at least make it one.
  • Andy’s injury report. Metcalf is on bye and Skattebo is questionable, but Breece Hall is back. The real worry is Tuten: he and Hubbard (24.9) carried Andy past Jake, but Tuten (29.9) is questionable with an ankle injury for tonight’s game in Baltimore.
